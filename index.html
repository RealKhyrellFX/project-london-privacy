Privacy Policy

How Project London plans to handle personal information.

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Current and planned features

This policy distinguishes the current development build from features planned for a future online release. Planned features described below are not necessarily available yet. This policy must be reviewed and updated before those features launch to reflect the systems and data practices actually used.

1. Who operates Project London?

Project London is an independently developed game operated by Khyrell DaCosta Haughton. It is not currently described as being operated by a registered company.

For privacy questions or requests concerning personal information, contact: khyrellhaughtond@gmail.com

2. The current development build

The current development build has been described as running locally and not collecting personal information itself. This statement applies to that build as currently understood, not automatically to future online versions or services provided by other companies.

The planned online release will require an internet connection. Online accounts, multiplayer and other connected features are planned but may not yet be available.

3. Information planned for collection

When planned online features are introduced, the information processed may include the following, depending on the feature used:

  • Account information: a username or display name, email address and date of birth where required for age-related purposes.
  • Platform account information: information made available through supported sign-in services, such as Xbox or Steam. Sign-in is planned to use supported platform services rather than a separate Project London password.
  • Technical information: IP addresses and relevant device or connection information for online connectivity, security and troubleshooting.
  • Game information: gameplay progress, saved data and information needed to provide multiplayer or cross-platform features.
  • Communications and moderation: information necessary to support planned text chat, live voice chat, player reports and blocking features. The precise handling of voice-chat data will be confirmed before that feature launches.

Project London does not plan to request GPS location or allow players to upload their own content through the game. IP addresses may nevertheless provide an approximate indication of a connection's location.

4. Why information may be used

Subject to the features actually launched and the applicable requirements, information may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain player accounts and sign-in.
  • Operate multiplayer and cross-platform features.
  • Save and synchronise gameplay progress.
  • Help secure the game and investigate technical problems.
  • Support player reporting, blocking and moderation.
  • Apply age-related restrictions where required.
  • Process optional purchases or downloadable content.
  • Provide contextual in-game advertising, if introduced.

The lawful basis for each type of processing will be confirmed before the relevant online feature launches. Information will not be used for purposes incompatible with the applicable requirements.

5. Xbox, Steam and other service providers

Project London plans to support platforms including Xbox, Windows PC through Microsoft Store, and Steam. These platforms may process account, purchase, technical or gameplay information under their own privacy policies.

Project London may receive information from a platform when needed to provide a supported feature. The information made available depends on the platform, the feature and the applicable settings.

The exact online service arrangements have not all been finalised. The policy will be updated to identify relevant providers and explain their roles once those arrangements are confirmed.

6. Storage and security

Planned gameplay saves may be stored on a player's device and through supported platform cloud services. Online features may also require service providers to process technical or gameplay information. The final storage arrangements will be explained once the systems are selected.

Planned security measures include platform sign-in security and encrypted connections when information is transmitted. Appropriate measures will depend on the systems actually used. No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. How long information is kept

The planned approach is to keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose for which it was collected, including applicable security, support and legal requirements. Specific retention periods have not yet been finalised and will be established before the relevant features launch.

8. Advertising and purchases

Optional in-game purchases or downloadable content and contextual advertising are being considered for the planned release. Contextual advertising is intended not to be targeted using personal information. The advertising provider and its data practices have not yet been confirmed.

Before advertising or purchases launch, this policy will be updated to explain the actual arrangements, including any information processed by the relevant providers. Any required choices or permissions will be provided where applicable.

9. Chat and player safety

Text chat and live voice chat are planned features. Reporting and blocking players are also planned. Information associated with these features may be processed where necessary to operate the service and address security or moderation concerns.

The voice-chat recording policy and any moderation-related storage arrangements must be confirmed before voice chat launches. Project London will not make a promise about recording or retention until the final technical arrangements are known.

10. International processing

Depending on the platforms and providers used, information may be processed outside the United Kingdom. The locations and applicable transfer arrangements will be checked before the relevant services launch. Where required, appropriate legal safeguards will be used for international transfers.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on the applicable law and circumstances, you may have rights to request access to your personal information, ask for inaccurate information to be corrected, request deletion or restriction of processing, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a request, email khyrellhaughtond@gmail.com . Please explain your request and provide enough information for it to be handled securely. You should not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information by email.

Project London plans to support requests for access to personal information and deletion of relevant data. The exact account deletion process is not yet implemented and will be explained when the online account system is introduced.

You may also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Guidance is available at ico.org.uk.

12. Children and age-related privacy

Project London is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children. A date of birth may be used for age-related restrictions where needed.

The game may nevertheless be accessed by younger players. Before online features launch, the relevant age-related safeguards and handling of children's information will be reviewed against applicable data protection requirements.

13. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as Project London develops. The latest version will be published on this website, and significant changes will be announced through the game or official community channels where appropriate.

The date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes. If a change requires additional notice or action under applicable law, that will be handled accordingly.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests or deletion requests, contact the Project London operator at:

khyrellhaughtond@gmail.com
